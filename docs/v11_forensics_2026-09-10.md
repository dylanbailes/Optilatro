## 3. Failure anatomy (frozen V10 on 10500–10799, 258 deaths)

| apex | finding |
|---|---|
| Death blind kind | Boss 143, **Big 79, Small 36** — 45% of deaths are on non-boss blinds |
| Death ante | 3:37 4:55 5:60 6:31 7:22 8:12 (+ 41 in antes 1–2) |
| Ante-8 conversion | 77.8% of arrivals win → **depth, not the final boss, is the bottleneck** |
| Money at death | mean **$8.1** (V11 rewrite: $9.3) — idle cash exists but is small, ~1–2 jokers |
| Winning-run profile | spent $323 vs $148 overall; econ $34.2 vs $17.1; planets 14.0 vs 4.9 |

Root-caused by replay (10510 / 10501 / 10511, all Ante-3 Big, target 3,000):

```
=== seed 10510 ANTE3 BIG target 3000 hands 4 disc 4 jokers 5  $13
   DISCARD 5D / AD / QH / QS      <- all four discards on SINGLE cards
   play Two Pair +112 -> 112
   play Full House +324 -> 436
   play Pair +52 -> 488
   play High Card +280 -> 768     >>> GAME OVER
```

The urgency model compares an optimistic `_forecast_round_score` against
**boss × 2.0** and only trims the interest target to $15/$10 in antes 2–5, with
`reroll_max` 2–3. So the agent banks ~$13, sees at most two extra shop cards,
enters a 3,000-chip Big Blind it cannot beat, burns every discard on single-card
value farming, and dies holding the cash. `forecast_score < next_blind_target`
essentially never fires, which is why the forecast-gated "desperation" trigger
was inert (byte-identical to control).

All on the pure-V10-delegating base via `bench/bench_v11_ablation.py`.

| arm | wins | net vs V10 | verdict |

The interest response is non-monotonic (20 → 11, 15 → 14, 12 → 9) — i.e. **n=100
cannot resolve a ±3-win effect at this win rate**. The decisive n=300 run settled
it: `shipped` (interest $15) = **35/300, gained 11 / lost 18 = −7** against frozen
V10's 42/300. The 100-seed screen was noise. Component G is therefore **dormant**
(`v11_interest_target = None`); the `_v11_shop_overrides` plumbing it needed is
kept because it is the only way to give V11 a different economic policy without
editing the frozen `agent_v10.py`.

**Screening lesson:** at ~12 wins/100 a 100-seed screen can only detect effects
larger than ~5 wins; the per-arm noise floor is ±3. Adopt nothing on n=100.

## 5. Component I: additive open-slot counterfactual search — INERT

`SearchShopV10._search_shop` runs its human-fair `ΔV = V(s') − V(s)` search
**only when joker slots are full and ante ≥ 4**. Open slots fall straight through
to the pure heuristic with no search. Since every historical gain came from
search, this was the obvious remaining dimension, so `_v11_open_slot_search` was
built to be purely additive (it can only override a `leave_shop`/`reroll` answer,
never displace a calibrated buy), using the same
`formulate_counterfactual_state` / `evaluate_shop_value` machinery.

| arm | wins/100 | flips |
|---|---|---|
| `l1_parity` (frozen V10) | 12 | 0 / 0 |
| `oss02` (ΔV > 0.02) | 12 | 0 / 0 (identical spend/bought/sold) |
| `oss10`, `oss20` | 12 | byte-identical to control |

At the 0.02 hurdle the search fires only rarely and changes nothing measurable;
at 0.10/0.20 it never fires (`evaluate_shop_value` simply never produces a 0.10
ΔV for an open-slot joker purchase). **Inert, left dormant.**

## 6. Open lever (unchanged, not yet tried)

Nothing in the knob space moves this policy any more — three independent rounds
(this one, plus the iters recorded in `STATUS.md`) now agree. Reaching 20%
requires a *qualitatively* stronger human-fair search, not another parameter:
- `evaluate_shop_value` (the linear/MLP model behind the ΔV search) is the
  bottleneck. Its open-slot ΔV never clears 0.10, so it cannot rank candidate
  jokers usefully — the model, not the search gate, is what to fix.
- A human-fair **shop-tree** search (multi-item hypothetical sequences evaluated
  by rollout with composition-sampled draws, as in the rejected
  `_v10_sampled_pick`) or a policy trained on rollout outcomes.

`candidate_cap` is a dead parameter in `agent_v10.py` (only `agent_l1.py` reads
it), so widening it there does nothing.
